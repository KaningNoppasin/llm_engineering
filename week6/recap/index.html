Week 6 — data, baselines & fine-tuning

Five daily recaps with active-recall quizzes. The capstone begins: predict a price.

The week in one line: curate the dataset (Day 1) → a 5-step process & cheap batch preprocessing (Day 2) → traditional ML baselines (Day 3) → a PyTorch net vs zero-shot frontier models (Day 4) → fine-tuning, why it failed here, and a from-scratch redemption (Day 5).

The five lessons

  • Day 1 — Curating a training dataset. Generalization, leakage, weighted sampling, splits, push to the Hub.
  • Day 2 — The 5-step process & batch processing. Understand→Productionize, MLOps, JSONL batch, 22k calls for <$1.
  • Day 3 — Traditional ML baselines. Random→constant→linear→Bag-of-Words→Random Forest→XGBoost.
  • Day 4 — Neural nets vs frontier models. PyTorch training loop, human baseline, zero-shot LLMs win.
  • Day 5 — Fine-tuning (and when it fails). SFT, JSONL, why it got worse, when to use RAG/prompt instead.
