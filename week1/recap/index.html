Week 1 — the foundations

Five daily recaps, each with an active-recall quiz. Built for the career switch into AI engineering.

The week in one line: from a single API call (Day 1) → many providers behind one client (Day 2) → what kinds of models exist (Day 3) → how they actually work (Day 4) → wiring calls into a real product (Day 5).

The five lessons

  • Day 1 — Your first LLM call & the web summarizer. Messages, system vs user prompts, .env, Ollama vs cloud.
  • Day 2 — One client, many providers. The endpoint under the hood; OpenAI-compatible Gemini & Ollama; open vs frontier; distillation.
  • Day 3 — Base, chat & reasoning models. The taxonomy, RLHF, hallucination, agentic AI.
  • Day 4 — Transformers, tokens & the illusion of memory. Attention, parameters, tiktoken, statelessness.
  • Day 5 — Prompt chaining, JSON & streaming. The brochure generator, structured output, streaming.
