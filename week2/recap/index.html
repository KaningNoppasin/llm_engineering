  • Day 3 — Chatbots & prompt steering. gr.ChatInterface, the messages recipe, multi-shot, primitive RAG.
  • Day 4 — Tool calling, no magic. The round-trip, the schema, finish_reason, the while loop.
  • Day 5 — Multimodal & agentic assistants. DALL·E 3, TTS, gr.Blocks, what makes it an agent.
