Week 5 — RAG

Five daily recaps with active-recall quizzes. The week you build "chat with your docs."

The week in one line: RAG & embeddings (Day 1) → chunking into a vector store (Day 2) → the full retriever + LLM pipeline (Day 3) → measuring it with MRR & an LLM judge (Day 4) → advanced techniques that lifted MRR 0.73 → 0.91 (Day 5).

The five lessons

  • Day 1 — RAG & vector embeddings. Why RAG, naive keyword lookup, encoders, semantic similarity, word2vec.
  • Day 2 — Chunking & vector stores. Text splitters, overlap, embedding models, Chroma/FAISS, t-SNE.
  • Day 3 — The full RAG pipeline. Retriever + LLM + memory, top-k, the multi-turn retrieval bug, Gradio.
  • Day 4 — Evaluating RAG. MRR, nDCG, recall, LLM-as-judge, golden data, iterating chunk size & embeddings.
  • Day 5 — Advanced RAG. Semantic chunking, query rewriting/expansion, re-ranking, GraphRAG.
